## Narrative state

Track the listener's knowledge after every bar:

```yaml
bar: 5
new_fact: ""
belief_created: ""
fact_withheld: ""
emotion_shift: ""
speaker_goal: ""
obstacle: ""
open_loop_ids: []
closed_loop_ids: []
```

Every bar need not add a fact, but a four-bar unit must change information, emotion, or flow. Use
texture bars deliberately and label them.

Build a verse from:

- proposition: the claim that survives compression;
- pressure: what makes the claim costly or difficult;
- turn: the new fact, choice, or interpretation;
- residue: the image, question, or consequence left after the final bar.

## Setup and payoff design

A setup creates a recoverable expectation. Record:

- literal situation;
- likely audience prediction;
- cue words or register;
- information intentionally withheld;
- acceptable distance to payoff;
- payoff id.

A payoff must transform at least one setup element through revelation, reversal, reinterpretation,
scale change, register collision, or consequence. Mere rhyme surprise is not a payoff.

## Punchline construction

Draft in this order:

1. write the non-rhyming proposition;
2. identify the expected completion;
3. choose the violated dimension: identity, scale, cause, status, time, register, or reference;
4. find a concrete reveal;
5. position only the essential cue before the reveal;
6. design silence and prominence;
7. add rhyme without moving the reveal into unnatural Japanese;
8. test whether the line remains interesting without its rhyme.

Prefer a punchline that performs two jobs: comic reveal plus character, plot, theme, or emotional
cost.

## Callback and escalation

A callback repeats an element with changed meaning. Track:

- first appearance and initial interpretation;
- intervening information;
- repeated or transformed surface;
- new interpretation;
- payoff strength if the audience misses the original.

Escalation can increase scale, specificity, consequence, social exposure, or emotional honesty.
Do not escalate only by adding louder adjectives.

## Character knowledge

For dialogue or multiple voices, maintain a knowledge matrix:

| Fact | Narrator | Character A | Character B | Listener |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| hidden debt | knows | knows | does not know | learns at B8 |

Comedy from recognition gaps works when wording and voice show who misunderstands what. A random
voice switch without information asymmetry is costume, not structure.

## Failure patterns

Reject or rewrite:

- a punchline with no identifiable expectation;
- reference-name substitution where recognition is the entire joke;
- a setup explained so fully that no discovery remains;
- a payoff whose syntax is forced by the rhyme;
- repeated self-deprecation with no new consequence or insight;
- punch-down at a less powerful target;
- four unrelated quotable lines with no narrative carry;
- an emotional turn announced by abstract nouns rather than caused by an event.
