# Technique Mixer

## Purpose

Turn research observations into independent controls. Never request or output “R-指定風,”
“GADORO風,” or another artist clone. Use named artists only as evidence sources during analysis;
remove their names from the generation brief.

## Trait normalization

Convert observations into this form:

```yaml
technique_id: H-INCONGRUITY-REGISTER-01
layer: humor
operation: "prestige register applied to a mundane object"
input_conditions:
  - "speaker performs status"
  - "concrete low-stakes object is available"
expected_effect:
  - "self-directed status reversal"
dependencies:
  - "setup creates prestige expectation"
incompatible_with:
  - "fully sincere climax at the same beat"
evidence:
  type: observed | artist_stated | scholarly
  source: ""
confidence: 0.0
imitation_risk: low | medium | high
```

Keep `artist_stated`, `critic/interviewer interpretation`, and `listener observation` separate.

## Module palette

Choose at most one primary and one supporting control from each relevant layer:

- narrative: scene, confession, argument, dialogue, montage, delayed reveal;
- vocabulary: object-led, action-led, institutional, domestic, technical, bodily, regional;
- humor: mechanism plus target plus timing;
- rhyme: family architecture, placement architecture, relation type, sound texture;
- flow: density contour, repeated cell, switch point, rest strategy, voice;
- hook: contradiction, command, question, object motif, crowd response;
- production: space, drum feel, arrangement contour, vocal treatment.

Do not maximize every control. A high-density internal rhyme chain, layered double meaning,
character dialogue, and multiple callbacks can compete for listener attention.

## Combination protocol

1. Lock proposition, pressure, turn, and residue.
2. Select a concrete vocabulary field.
3. Choose one humor mechanism only if the brief benefits from humor.
4. Select a rhyme architecture that supports important word pairs.
5. Select a flow contour that gives the reveal and turn enough space.
6. Check compatibility. Remove the lowest-priority technique when two controls obscure meaning.
7. Generate distinct seeds from meaning, sound, flow, and hook.
8. Integrate only techniques traceable to the selected controls.
9. Run imitation review: remove signature phrases, biography, ad-libs, exact combinations, and
   recognizable scene reconstruction.

## Diversity controls

Across a verse, vary:

- rhyme placement rather than only rhyme family;
- sentence length and clause opening;
- concreteness and semantic domain;
- setup distance;
- voice and emotional temperature;
- information density;
- exact, slant, and non-rhyme texture.

Do not force variety into every bar. Establish patterns before breaking them.

## Evidence-to-generation firewall

Research artifacts may contain names, work titles, interviews, and observations. Generation
artifacts may contain only normalized techniques, constraints, and original material. The Draft
Integrator must not receive commercial lyric text or artist-specific signature phrases.
